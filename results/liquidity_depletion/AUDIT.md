# Liquidity depletion repository and data audit

- The frozen balanced entry point is `strategies/competition_strategy.py`, inheriting `final_strategy.py` and `our_strategy.py`. `_maintain_quote` keeps or cancels resting quotes; `_can_send` centralizes position, worst-case weighted exposure, order count, message reserve, and self-trade checks. This project must subclass the wrapper, not alter it.
- Existing causal book features live in `strategies/orderflow_imbalance.py`; `research/data_loader.py` loads typed CSVs. `strategies/queue_execution.py` and `queue_strategy.py` contain experimental queue-priority heuristics and order management. They do not reveal actual queue position, and the frozen balanced strategy does not use them.
- `data/<session>.top.csv` records a snapshot at the **end** of each 500 ms batch for each of HPPO, BNKY, CRRT, BRRY. Best prices are integer cents; best quantities are aggregate displayed shares. Blank cells mean an empty side, not zero. Historical status is always `TRADING`; live states may halt or close. A best-price transition replaces the observed queue, so old and new quantities are not directly comparable.
- `data/<session>.trades.csv` records executions during that batch; `aggressor=BUY` is an arriving buy consuming an ask and `SELL` is an arriving sell consuming a bid. Trade `price` is the resting execution price. The tape is published alongside the end-of-batch top. Multiple executions, additions, cancellations, and price changes may occur within one batch. A trade at the previous ask/bid is only a **coincident proxy** for consumption of that queue. Exact cancelled quantity, replenishment source, hidden liquidity, and event order cannot be reconstructed from these two files.
- The SDK backtester processes messages sent after batch `b` in `b+1`, then replays resting-order fills from that batch's trades, then exposes the end-of-batch top and trade tape to `on_batch`. The recorded public top never includes our simulated orders. In a live exchange, our orders can enter the visible top; without deeper levels, subtracting our size cannot reliably reveal the external best after our quote creates it. Live-style features must abstain or reset when own orders may contaminate the touch.
- The SDK imposes 20 trading messages per batch, 40 resting orders per symbol, ±600 weighted exposure, per-symbol limits, and maker/taker fees. Its replay handles cancel acknowledgments and partial fills but lacks our book impact, true queue position, competing market-maker reactions, and hidden true-value scoring. All study comparisons use s01–s16 training and s17–s20 previously used validation; s21–s24 were previously examined and are not opened for tuning. Research labels may look forward; live features may not.
